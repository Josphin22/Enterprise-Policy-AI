ENTERPRISE WORK FROM HOME AND REMOTE WORK POLICY

Section 1: Remote Work Eligibility
Full-time staff members who have completed their initial 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely up to 2 days per week with direct manager approval. Employees on performance improvement plans are not eligible for remote work.

Section 2: Workspace Security & VPN Protocols
Employees working remotely must maintain a secure, private ergonomic workspace. Remote workers must connect through the corporate VPN at all times and ensure confidential proprietary company information remains protected against unauthorized access.
