ENTERPRISE ATTENDANCE AND WORKING HOURS POLICY

Section 1: Standard Daily Schedule
Standard enterprise working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday, totaling a 40-hour work week. All employees are required to record daily check-in and check-out timestamps through the enterprise portal.

Section 2: Mandatory Core Hours
Core working hours during which all team members must be actively available for meetings and synchronous team collaboration are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Flexible arrival and departure times are permitted outside core hours with supervisor approval.
