ENTERPRISE EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS

Section 1: Professional Conduct and Non-Discrimination
All employees must maintain the highest standards of professional integrity, mutual respect, and ethical conduct. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation in any form is subject to immediate disciplinary action up to termination.

Section 2: Resignation Notice Requirements
Employees in regular individual contributor positions must provide a minimum of 30 calendar days written advance notice for voluntary resignation. Employees in management or executive roles must provide 60 calendar days advance notice.
