ENTERPRISE CORPORATE TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Section 1: Domestic Meal Allowances
The maximum domestic meal reimbursement allowance is $75 per day with itemized receipts. Individual meal guidelines are $20 for breakfast, $25 for lunch, and $30 for dinner. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable.

Section 2: Expense Report Submission Timelines
All travel expense reports and itemized receipts must be submitted through the finance portal within 14 calendar days following the completion of business travel. Late expense filings require written authorization from the Department Vice President.
