ENTERPRISE IT SECURITY AND ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

Section 1: Password Complexity and Expiration Standards
All corporate user passwords must be at least 12 characters in length, containing uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Passwords must be changed every 90 days, and previous 5 passwords cannot be reused.

Section 2: Device Encryption and USB Usage
All employee laptops and workstations must have BitLocker full-disk encryption enabled. Unencrypted USB storage devices are strictly prohibited on enterprise hardware.
