ENTERPRISE ANNUAL AND SICK LEAVE POLICY

Section 1: Annual Vacation Leave Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of annual paid vacation leave per calendar year. Leave accrues on a pro-rata basis at 1.25 days per month of continuous service. Part-time employees receive leave prorated to their weekly contracted working hours.

Section 2: Leave Carry Forward & Expiration
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual vacation days into the subsequent calendar year. Any additional unused leave beyond the 5-day limit will automatically lapse on December 31 of each year.

Section 3: Sick Leave Allotment
Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually on January 1. Sick leave does not accumulate year-over-year. A certified medical practitioner note is mandatory for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive business days.
